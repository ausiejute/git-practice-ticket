Fun fact about London: The Great Beer Flood of 1814: A massive vat containing 388,000 imperial gallons of beer ruptured at the Meux and Company Brewery in Tottenham. The explosion sent a tidal wave of beer cascading down the streets, demolishing two houses and killing at least 8 people. The brewery was taken to court... and found not guilty because the court ruled it was an "Act of God."
